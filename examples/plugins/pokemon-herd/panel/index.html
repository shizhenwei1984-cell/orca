Pokémon Herd

How to play

Meet your companions

Each agent has a Pokémon. Click it in the World to focus its terminal. Right-click it, or press Shift+F10 with it focused, to pet it, use an item, or teach moves. Agent work earns Pokédollars and can trigger evolution.

Catch wild Pokémon

Click a visitor to collect its gift, then again to throw a ball. Set your ball or Berry in World actions. Drag a companion onto the visitor to weaken it, or use the encounter’s Battle controls. Low health helps you catch it. Stop before the visitor faints.

Prepare for battle

Use Teach moves on a companion or a Trainer Party or Box card. Keep up to four moves and save them for the selected form. Use Reset to default to restore its original set. Turn on Choose moves against trainers to pick attacks yourself.

Use Spar in the encounter controls to train with another companion. When a raid egg hatches, send a companion against the boss. Defeat it for a chance to catch it.

Explore the Trainer pages

Open Trainer or press B with the World focused. Buy balls in Shop, bring a Pokémon out of the Box, or complete all three daily Research tasks to earn Ultra Balls and an Azure Flute that calls a legendary visitor.

Make the Herd yours

Use Settings to adjust scenery, visitor frequency, evolution, and sounds. Trainer mode limits new companions to caught species and the Kanto starters. Press S with the World focused to reopen Settings.
